# Character Guide — xưng hô & Speech Patterns

Characters are grouped by how distinctive their speech is. High-priority characters have detailed profiles; secondary characters have concise notes.

---

## TIER 1 — Highly distinctive speech (requires close attention)

### Beatrice / Betty (ベアトリス) — Arcs 2+

**Self:** "ta" (archaic) OR "Betty" (third-person self-reference — keep "Betty")
**Calls Subaru:** "ngươi" (archaic pair to "ta") or "cậu" (softer moments)
**Calls groups:** "các ngươi"

**Sentence-final markers — non-negotiable:**
| Japanese | English | Vietnamese |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| ~かしら | I suppose | "…nhỉ?" / "…vậy nhỉ?" |
| ~のよ / ~わよ | in fact | "…đấy chứ" / "…cơ mà" |
| ~わ | soft assertion | "…mà" / "…đó" |
| ~でしょう | obviously | "…chứ" / "…rõ ràng là vậy mà" |

**Tone:** Haughty and imperious, but her care for Subaru must be legible underneath. She will never admit it directly.

**Correct example:**
> Beatrice: "Subaru? Có chuyện gì vậy nhỉ? Vẻ mặt của cậu trông hơi lạ đấy chứ."

**Incorrect (stripped of markers):**
> Beatrice: "Subaru? Có chuyện gì vậy? Vẻ mặt của cậu trông hơi lạ."

### Roswaal L. Mathers (ロズワール・L・メイザース) — Arcs 2+

**Self:** "ta" (theatrical archaic)
**Calls Subaru:** "cậu" or respectful titles depending on context
**Speech style:** Extremely theatrical. In Japanese, he elongates vowels dramatically (~ぁ, ~ぉ). Render this in Vietnamese with a slight elongation effect — not literally "~" but through word choice and sentence rhythm that feels performative and drawn-out.
**Tone:** He is always playing a role. His words mean multiple things simultaneously. Never let him sound direct or sincere unless the scene specifically breaks his facade.

---

### Priscilla Barielle (プリシラ・バリエル) — Arc 3+

**Self:** "ta" (royally haughty)
**Speech style:** Everything exists to serve her. She never doubts herself. Extreme condescension toward everyone, but stated as calm fact rather than insult. The world literally bends to her will.
**Tone:** Absolute confidence. She is not angry when dismissive — she is simply stating the natural order of things.

**Example:**
> Priscilla: "Hiển nhiên là vậy. Ta không bao giờ sai."

---

### Garfiel Tinsel (ガーフィール・ティンゼル) — Arc 4+

**Self:** "tao" (rough, working-class)
**Calls others:** "mày" (peers/enemies), shifts to "cậu" for those he respects
**Speech style:** Rough, aggressive surface tone that masks genuine warmth and loyalty. Boastful — he brags about his own amazing self. But the brag is sincere, not ironic.
**Tone:** Loud and confident on the outside, emotionally earnest underneath. His dialect should feel village-rough, not educated-urban.

---

### Regulus Corneas (レグルス・コーニアス) — Arc 5

**Self:** "tôi" (calm, formal — never inflated)
**Speech style:** EXTREMELY long monologues about his rights, his peace, how everyone else is wrong to inconvenience him. The length and calmness are intentional characterisation.
**Tone:** Never raise the emotional register. He is not angry — he is perpetually disappointed and self-justified. Do NOT summarize his monologues. Translate them in full, no matter how long.

---

### Ferris / Felix Argyle (フェリス・アーガイル) — Arc 3+

**Self:** "Ferri" (third-person self-reference, feminine)
**Speech style:** Feminine register throughout. In Japanese uses "-nya" suffix. Vietnamese equivalent: sentence-final "nha~", "nè", refers to self as "Ferri".
**Tone:** Playful and cute externally, but fiercely protective of Crusch. The cuteness is not a mask — it is genuine — but do not let it erase the steel underneath.

**Example:**
> Ferris: "Ối, Ferri không muốn làm chuyện đó đâu nha~"

## TIER 2 — Notable speech patterns (important but less complex)

### Natsuki Subaru (ナツキ・スバル) — All arcs

**Self:** "tôi" (default), "mình" (intimate), "ta" only for deliberate sarcasm
**Calls others:** by name; "cậu" (peers), "anh/chị" (perceived older), "em" (younger)
**Speech style:** Casual, fast-paced, modern pop-culture references that land awkwardly in a fantasy world. Self-deprecating humour. Rambling internal monologue. Not polished — never sounds like a noble or knight.
**Tone:** Energetic when optimistic, blunt when serious, tries to be funny when scared. When determined, the jokes drop completely.

---

### Emilia (エミリア) — All arcs

**Self:** "tôi"
**Calls Subaru:** "Subaru"
**Speech style:** Warm, sincere, occasionally naive. Takes things literally. Slightly formal for royal affairs; otherwise natural and gentle.
**Tone:** Never sarcastic. Never dismissive. When uncertain, she says so honestly. Her lines feel like sunlight — clear and warm.

---

### Rem (レム) — Arc 2+

**Self:** "tôi" (formal), occasionally "Rem" (self-reference in deeply emotional scenes)
**Calls Subaru:** "Subaru-kun" → translate as "Subaru" or "anh Subaru" depending on intimacy level of the scene
**Speech style:** Gentle, devoted, proper. In battle she is fierce and direct. In emotional scenes, her devotion breaks through the formality.
**Tone:** She means everything she says. No irony, no games. Her love for Subaru and her sister shapes every line.

---

### Ram (ラム) — Arc 2+

**Self:** "tôi"
**Calls Subaru:** "cậu" or just his name
**Speech style:** Curt, proud, occasionally condescending. Minimal words — she says what is necessary and stops. Short sentences, perfect timing for cutting remarks.
**Tone:** No filler words. Every Ram line should feel like it could be shorter and still land.

---

### Otto Suwen (オットー・スーウェン) — Arc 4+

**Self:** "tôi"
**Speech style:** Rapid, nervous, exasperated, but fundamentally practical. The straight-man against chaos. His panic is comedic but his advice is often the correct call.
**Tone:** He sounds like a merchant trying to survive a fantasy novel he did not sign up for. Keep the exasperation — it is his defining quality.

---

### Julius Juukulius (ユリウス・ユークリウス) — Arc 3+

**Self:** "tôi"
**Calls Subaru:** "cậu", occasionally "Subaru"
**Speech style:** Eloquent, formal, knightly. He chooses words with care. Never colloquial, never slang. Can sound slightly pompous.
**Tone:** Precise vocabulary. Longer, well-structured sentences.

---

### Anastasia Hoshin (アナスタシア・ホーシン) — Arc 3+

**Self:** "tôi"
**Speech style:** Merchant queen — warm, clever, slightly theatrical. In Japanese she uses a Kansai dialect to signal her merchant-class origins. Capture this as: slightly more relaxed and regional in feel than formal court speech, with a cunning undertone. She is always calculating even when she sounds casual.
**Tone:** She never shows her full hand. Her warmth is genuine but strategic.

---

### Crusch Karsten (クルシュ・カルステン) — Arc 3+

**Self:** "tôi"
**Speech style:** Formal, military, direct. A general's bearing. Principled and to-the-point.
**Tone:** Commanding without being cold. She respects those who earn it.

---

### Reinhard van Astrea (ラインハルト・ヴァン・アストレア) — Arc 1+

**Self:** "tôi"
**Speech style:** Polite, earnest, genuinely kind. Unintentionally intimidating because of his incomprehensible power. He is not showing off — he simply is what he is.
**Tone:** Warmth without irony. He is the ideal knight who happens to actually exist.

---

### Wilhelm van Astrea (ウィルヘルム・ヴァン・アストレア) — Arc 3+

**Self:** "tôi"
**Speech style:** Formal veteran warrior. Economical and respectful. His devotion to his wife (Theresia) is the emotional undercurrent of every scene.
**Tone:** Quiet dignity. He does not boast. He simply acts.

---

### Felt (フェルト) — Arc 1+

**Self:** "tao" or "tớ" (street register)
**Speech style:** Rough, direct, no-nonsense. A thief's vocabulary, not a noble's. She does not moderate her speech for anyone.
**Tone:** She earned everything herself. That pride colours every line.

## TIER 3 — Arc 6 party

### Meili Portroute (メィリィ・ポートルート)

**Self:** "em"
**Calls others:** "anh/chị" + name
**Speech style:** Childlike, playful, mischievous. Sentence-final: "nè~", "đó nha", "ha~", "vậy á"
**Tone:** Cute surface, assassin underneath. Let the edge show when she talks about witch beasts.

### Echidna — Tower Sage (artificial spirit, contracted to Julius)

**Note:** NOT the Witch of Greed. Different character sharing the name.
**Self:** "tôi"
**Speech style:** Analytical, dry, matter-of-fact. Cuts to the point.

### Shaula (シャウラ)

**Self:** "ta" (battle) or "tôi" (casual)
**Speech style:** Energetic, passionate, almost reverential toward Sage Flugel.
**Tone:** High energy. Not refined. Devoted.

### Louis Arneb (ルイ・アルネブ) — Archbishop of Gluttony

**Self:** "tôi"/"mình" — shifts across Arc 6 as she absorbs/loses Subaru's memories
**Tone:** Handle with care — match the source text's cues about which personality is dominant.

## TIER 3 — Arc 7 / Vollachian Empire characters

### Abel / Vincent Vollachia (ヴィンセント・ヴォラキア) — Arc 7+

**Self:** "Ta" (haughty imperial). Occasionally slips into the royal **"Trẫm" (朕)** before quickly correcting himself to "Ta" — this slip is characterisation and MUST be preserved when it appears in the source.
**Calls Subaru:** "ngươi" (cold, commanding)
**Speech style:** Sharp, strategic, calculating. An emperor disguised as a wanderer. Every word is chosen with absolute precision. He speaks with the authority of one accustomed to absolute obedience.
**Tone:** Imperious condescension mixed with reluctant strategic respect. He does not ask — he commands or permits. When he calls Subaru by name instead of a dismissive term, it means something — preserve that shift.

**Possessive caution:** Abel uses "ta/ngươi", so a possessive about the *listener's* belongings must be "của ngươi", never "của ta" (see [mistakes.md Category 3.4b](mistakes.md)).

**Example:**
> Abel: "Kẻ ngu ngốc. Ai cho phép ngươi lên tiếng? Từ giờ trở đi, ngươi phải chọn lọc từng câu chữ cho thật cẩn thận. Đừng quên rằng mạng sống của ngươi đang nằm trong tay trẫm…… của ta đấy."

### Todd Fang (トッド・ファング) — Arc 7+

**Self:** "tôi" (casual/friendly) or "ta" (when asserting authority)
**Calls Subaru:** "cậu" (friendly) or "ngươi" (hostile)
**Speech style:** Deceptively warm and amiable on the surface. Beneath the friendly smile lies an ice-cold pragmatist who evaluates threats with terrifying rationality. His shift from friendly to lethal should feel like a chill running down the reader's spine.
**Tone:** When friendly — relaxed, conversational, almost brotherly. When hostile — clinical, matter-of-fact about killing, zero malice but absolute certainty.

---

### Jamal Aurélie (ジャマル・アウレリエ) — Arc 7+

**Self:** "tao" (vulgar, aggressive)
**Calls others:** "mày" (crude), "thằng ranh" / "thằng khốn" (insults for Subaru)
**Speech style:** Brutal, loud, short-tempered military bully. Coarse language. Zero patience. Expresses contempt through physical violence and foul language.
**Tone:** Pure aggression. He is Todd's opposite — where Todd calculates, Jamal rages.

---

### Mizelda (ミゼルダ) — Arc 7+ (Chieftain of the Shudraq)

**Self:** "Ta" (chieftain authority)
**Calls Subaru:** "nhóc con" (belittling) or "ngươi"
**Calls others:** "các ngươi" (addressing group)
**Speech style:** Bold, proud Amazonian warrior-chieftain. Direct, commanding, unapologetically fierce. Her authority comes from strength, not politics.
**Tone:** Speaks as someone who leads by combat prowess. Proud of her tribe. Does not defer to men or outsiders.

---

### Taritta (タリッタ) — Arc 7+ (Mizelda's sister)

**Self:** "tôi" or "em" (to Mizelda)
**Calls Mizelda:** "Chị" or "Tộc trưởng"
**Speech style:** Quieter and more reserved than Mizelda, but carries the same warrior pride. An exceptional archer.
**Tone:** Respectful deference to her sister, fierce confidence in her own skills.

---

### Utakata (ウタカタ) — Arc 7+

**Self:** "Uu" (childlike third-person self-reference)
**Calls others:** "Mii" (Mizelda), "Suu" (Subaru), "anh" (to Subaru)
**Speech style:** Simple, childlike vocabulary. Short sentences. Natural childlike Vietnamese particles: "đó nha", "nè", "á", "nha~", "trơn".
**Tone:** Genuine innocent curiosity. Not a facade — she is truly a child exploring the world.

**Example:**
> Utakata: "Mii bảo không được nghe lời đàn ông. Nhưng Uu tò mò lắm. Tò mò về anh á."

---

### Clind (クリンド) — Arc 5+

**Self:** "tôi"
**Calls others:** "cậu"
**Speech style:** Impeccable butler formality. His unique trait is ending dialogues with a single concise word or phrase that serves as a terse summary/judgment of the situation. These must be translated as punchy, impactful Sino-Vietnamese or concise Vietnamese words.
**Signature endings:** *Very poor* → **Yếu kém.** / *Meddlesome* → **Bao đồng.** / *Inspired* → **Hào hứng.** / *Versatile* → **Toàn năng.**

---

### Cecilus Segmunt (セシルス・セグムント) — Arc 7+

**Self:** "tôi" or "ta" (in battle)
**Speech style:** Flamboyant, theatrical, almost musical in his joy of combat. He treats fighting like performance art and speaks with dramatic flair.
**Tone:** Carefree and dazzling, as if everything is a stage. Genuinely delighted by strong opponents.

---

### Medium O'Connell (ミディアム・オコーネル) — Arc 7+

**Self:** "tôi"
**Speech style:** Bright, energetic, straightforward. She says what she means without guile. Supportive and encouraging.
**Tone:** Sunshine energy, but not naive — she has genuine resolve beneath the cheerfulness.

---

### Yorna Mishigure (ヨルナ・ミシグレ) — Arc 7+

**Self:** "tôi" or "thiếp" (elegant feminine)
**Speech style:** Elegant fox-woman with maternal authority. She rules her domain with grace and protects those under her care with fierce devotion.
**Tone:** Regal warmth. She is a ruler who genuinely loves her people.

---

### Olbart Dunkelkenn (オルバルト・ダンケルケン) — Arc 7+

**Self:** "ta" or "lão" (crotchety old man)
**Speech style:** Mischievous, crotchety shinobi master. Speaks in the register of a crafty old man who enjoys toying with younger generations.
**Tone:** Playful cruelty mixed with genuine old-man cantankerousness.

---

### Aldebaran / Al (アルデバラン) — Arc 5+

**Self:** "tôi" or "ta" (casual-rough)
**Speech style:** Sarcastic, world-weary, deliberately casual to mask deep pain. Japanese "ore" energy. He deflects serious topics with humor.
**Tone:** A man who has seen too much and copes through detachment and sarcasm. When he drops the act, the weight is enormous.
