# Character Guide — xưng hô & Speech Patterns

## Natsuki Subaru (ナツキ・スバル)

**Self:** "tôi" (default), "mình" (intimate/casual moments), rarely "ta" (only for deliberate sarcasm)
**Calls others:** by name; "cậu" (peers), "anh/chị" (perceived as older), "em" (younger)
**Speech style:** Casual, energetic, fast-paced. Makes modern pop-culture references that land awkwardly in a fantasy world. Self-deprecating humour. Rambling internal monologue. Rough around the edges — he is not polished or noble.
**Tone guide:** Keep him lively and slightly informal. He never sounds like a trained knight. When he is scared, he still tries to be funny. When he is determined, the jokes drop and he is blunt.

---

## Emilia (エミリア)

**Self:** "tôi"
**Calls Subaru:** "Subaru"
**Speech style:** Warm, sincere, occasionally naive. She means everything she says and takes things literally. Slightly formal when discussing royal affairs; otherwise natural and gentle.
**Tone guide:** Never sarcastic. Never dismissive. When she is uncertain, she says so honestly. Her lines should feel like sunlight — clear and warm.

---

## Beatrice / Betty (ベアトリス)

**Self:** "ta" (archaic, haughty register) OR "Betty" (refers to herself in third person)
**Calls Subaru:** "ngươi" (archaic, matching "ta") or "cậu" (softer moments)
**Calls others (plural):** "các ngươi"

**Speech style:** Haughty, archaic, imperious — but clearly fond of Subaru beneath the surface. Her signature speech-final markers in Vietnamese:

| Japanese original | Vietnamese equivalent |
|------------------|-----------------------|
| ~かしら / I suppose | "…nhỉ?" or "…vậy nhỉ?" at sentence end |
| ~のよ / in fact | "…đấy chứ" or "…cơ mà" at sentence end |
| Betty (self-reference) | Keep "Betty" — do not replace with "ta" |
| ~わ (softened assertion) | "…mà" or "…đó" at sentence end |

**Tone guide:** These sentence-final markers are non-negotiable — they define Beatrice's voice. Do not smooth them into standard Vietnamese. She sounds archaic and imperious, but her care for Subaru should be legible.

**Example (correct):**
> Beatrice: "Subaru? Có chuyện gì vậy nhỉ? Vẻ mặt của cậu trông hơi lạ đấy chứ."

**Example (incorrect — stripped of markers):**
> Beatrice: "Subaru? Có chuyện gì vậy? Vẻ mặt của cậu trông hơi lạ."

## Julius Juukulius (ユリウス・ユークリウス)

**Self:** "tôi"
**Calls Subaru:** "cậu", occasionally "Subaru" by name
**Calls Meili:** "em" (as she positions herself as younger)
**Speech style:** Eloquent, formal, precise. The voice of a trained knight and nobleman. He chooses words carefully. Never colloquial, never slang. Can sound slightly pompous.
**Tone guide:** Precise vocabulary. Longer, well-constructed sentences. He never contracts or shortcuts his speech.

---

## Meili Portroute (メィリィ・ポートルート)

**Self:** "em" (consistently positions herself as younger sister to everyone)
**Calls Julius:** "anh Julius"
**Calls Subaru:** "anh Subaru" or just "anh"
**Speech style:** Childlike, playful, slightly mischievous. Uses:
- Trailing "~" on vowels for cuteness: "nè~", "ha~", "đúng không nè~?"
- Sentence-final: "nè", "đó nha", "ha", "vậy á"
- Speaks in a lilting, teasing register

**Tone guide:** She sounds cute but is a trained assassin. Let an occasional cold edge show through when she talks about her work with witch beasts. The contrast is intentional.

**Example (correct):**
> Meili: "ーーThế nên, mọi người mới nhận ra là nên gọi em đúng không nè~?"

---

## Echidna — Tower Sage (エキドナ, the artificial spirit contracted to Julius)

**Note:** This is NOT the Witch of Greed / Echidna from the Sanctuary arc. This is the artificial spirit bearing the same name, contracted to Julius Juukulius.

**Self:** "tôi"
**Speech style:** Analytical, dry, matter-of-fact. Economical with words. Little emotional inflection. States facts and assessments without embellishment.
**Tone guide:** She cuts to the point. Her lines are often the shortest in a scene. No rhetorical flourishes.

**Example (correct):**
> Echidna: "Trước kia, từng có một đội quân viễn chinh được phái đến với kế hoạch tiêu diệt bầy Ma Thú. Có điều, kết cục của họ hẳn đã quá rõ ràng qua âm thanh của những bước chân này rồi."

## Ram (ラム)

**Self:** "tôi"
**Calls Subaru:** "cậu" or just his name
**Speech style:** Curt, proud, slightly condescending. Minimal words. She says what is necessary and stops. Short sentences. Occasionally delivers cutting remarks with perfect calm.
**Tone guide:** No filler words. No long explanations unless the scene demands it. Every Ram line should feel like it could be shorter — and still landed perfectly.

---

## Shaula (シャウラ)

**Self:** "ta" (battle/formal mode) or "tôi" (casual)
**Speech style:** Energetic, passionate, almost reverential when speaking of the Sage Flugel. Uses exclamations naturally. High emotional volume.
**Tone guide:** Loud energy. She is not refined — she is devoted and powerful. Her affection for "master" (the Sage) borders on obsession.

---

## Louis Arneb (ルイ・アルネブ) — Archbishop of Gluttony

**Context:** Absorbed Subaru's memories. Her speech evolves across Arc 6 as she loses/regains pieces of identity.
**Self:** "tôi" or "mình" — shifts across the arc
**Tone guide:** Handle with care. Early in the arc she echoes Subaru's cadence; later she develops her own voice. Match the source text's cues about which personality is dominant at any given moment.

---

## Patrasche

Non-verbal. Ground dragon. Use action descriptions only — she communicates through movement and body language.

## Common xưng hô decisions

| Situation | Pronoun choice |
|-----------|---------------|
| Subaru → Emilia (talking about her) | "cô ấy" or "Emilia" |
| Subaru → Beatrice (talking about her) | "Betty" or "cô ấy" |
| Beatrice → group | "các ngươi" |
| Beatrice → Subaru (warm moments) | "cậu" (drop "ngươi") |
| Julius → Subaru | "cậu" |
| Meili → Julius | "anh Julius" |
| Ram → Subaru | "cậu" or name only |
| Narrator describing Subaru's thoughts | match the register of the scene |
